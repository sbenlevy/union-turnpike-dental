If you call us at (718) 217-0822, our staff explain before asking for your consent that you would receive appointment reminders, confirmations, and rescheduling notifications by text; that message frequency varies; that message and data rates may apply; that you can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help; that consent is not a condition of treatment; and that the full program terms are available at https://sbenlevy.github.io/union-turnpike-dental/terms.html.
