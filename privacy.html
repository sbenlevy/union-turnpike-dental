Union Turnpike Dental PC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Contact information you give us (such as your name, phone number, and email address), appointment information, and information you provide on our patient forms.

How we use information

To provide dental care, schedule and manage appointments, send appointment reminders and other service messages, respond to your questions, and meet our legal and professional obligations.

Text messaging

Our text messaging program, “Union Turnpike Dental PC Appointment Reminders,” sends appointment reminders, confirmations, and rescheduling notifications. Patients opt in in exactly two ways: (1) by completing an in-office paper form, or (2) verbally by phone after our staff explain the program. The paper form states: “By providing your mobile number and checking this box, you agree to receive SMS messages from Union Turnpike Dental PC, including appointment reminders, confirmations, and rescheduling notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of treatment. Full program terms: https://sbenlevy.github.io/union-turnpike-dental/terms.html.” The full program terms are at https://sbenlevy.github.io/union-turnpike-dental/terms.html.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or call us at (718) 217-0822.
